# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience, education, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- communicate with empathy, patience, and technical honesty;
- critique ideas and code without attacking people;
- accept constructive feedback and acknowledge mistakes;
- respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or security-sensitive data;
- prioritize the safety and long-term health of the community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, sustained disruption, and publishing private
communications without permission are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

For ordinary project disagreements, use the relevant issue or pull request.
For sensitive conduct reports, privately contact maintainers through a
[GitHub private security advisory](https://github.com/Bilyz26/Banking_System/security/advisories/new)
and state that the report concerns community conduct. Maintainers will review
reports confidentially, avoid conflicts of interest, and take proportionate
action up to temporary or permanent exclusion.

This policy applies in project spaces and when someone officially represents
the project elsewhere. It is inspired by the Contributor Covenant.
